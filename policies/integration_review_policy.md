# Third-Party Integration Review Policy

Revision 4

## Scope

This policy covers routing of third-party integration requests submitted to
the Integration Review Board.

## Routing

Requests that involve elevated data sensitivity, or that are granted
write-level access to internal systems, must be routed to **Security Review**.
All other requests receive a **Standard Review**.

## Data sensitivity reference

Every request records one data class:

- **Public** - information approved for external disclosure.
- **Internal** - routine business information.
- **Confidential** - commercially sensitive business information.
- **Restricted** - regulated data and highly sensitive data (customer PII,
  financial records, credentials).
